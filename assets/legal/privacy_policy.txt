BLIND TIGER — PRIVACY POLICY (PILOT)

Last updated: Pilot soft launch

The Blind Tiger Social Club ("we", "the club") operates the Blind Tiger mobile app and admin tools for members and staff during our venue pilot.

What we collect
• Account: name, email, birthdate (21+ verification), and club role.
• Visit data: time wallet balance, entry/exit timestamps, branch, session activity needed to run the time-based experience.
• Social & safety (optional): friend requests, presence when you opt in to "Open to Meet", safety reports, ride assist requests — only as you use those features.
• Staff operations: cash time loads, guest list, and safety desk records visible to authorized admin/staff.

How we use it
• To operate entry, your timer, rankings, tips, and social features you choose to use.
• To keep the venue safe (private reports to staff; reported members are not notified).
• We do not sell your personal data.

Sharing
• Service providers (e.g. cloud hosting/authentication) that process data on our behalf.
• Venue staff and authorized admins for operations and safety.
• Legal requirements when required by law.

Your choices
• You can opt out of social visibility by turning off Open to Meet.
• Contact the club desk to ask about account access or correction during the pilot.

Security
• We use industry-standard authentication and access controls. No system is 100% secure; report concerns to the club.

Contact
• Blind Tiger Social Club — ask staff at the venue or your pilot coordinator for privacy questions.

This is a simplified pilot notice, not legal advice. A full policy may replace this before public App Store launch.
