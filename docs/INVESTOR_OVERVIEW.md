# The Blind Tiger — Investor Overview

**Product brief for investors**  
*MVP stage · Pilot-ready · Cash-first*

---

## In one sentence

**The Blind Tiger** turns a night at the club into a living phone experience: guests buy **time**, spend it like currency inside the venue, and staff run the floor from a simple admin console — without waiting for full digital payments to be built.

---

## The problem we saw

Nightlife brands sell atmosphere, status, and belonging — but the guest’s phone usually only does tickets, chats, or Instagram.

At the door and the bar, operations still run on:

- cash and verbal agreements  
- paper guest lists  
- staff who can’t see who’s paid, who’s still inside, or how much time a guest has left  

That creates friction for guests and blind spots for the house. It also makes it hard to prove a modern, investable product story: *“We own the relationship with the member, not just the night.”*

---

## Our approach (why we built it this way)

### 1. Time is the product

Instead of only selling “entry,” we sell **club time**.

Guests load time onto their account. That time:

- fuels their visit  
- is spent on drinks and social moments in the lounge  
- shows up in ranking and VIP access  
- feels like a currency they can see moving on their phone  

**Why this matters:** time is easy to understand, hard to fake in the room, and naturally ties spending to presence.

### 2. Cash first, digital payments later

For the **pilot and investor demo**, we deliberately start with **cash at the desk**.

Staff (or admin) selects a standard bill — ₱1,000, ₱2,000, ₱3,000, and so on — and the guest’s phone wallet updates live.

**Why we deferred GCash / payment gateways for now:**

- We can run a real bar pilot without waiting on payment partner approvals  
- We keep the demo honest and controllable  
- We prove the *experience and operations* first — then add payment walls when the loop is validated  

Digital wallets and payment security hardening are **on the roadmap**, not missing by accident.

### 3. Two sides of the same product

| For guests (mobile app) | For the house (admin web) |
|-------------------------|---------------------------|
| Sign up, buy/load time, enter with QR | Dashboard of members & live visits |
| Lounge timer, menu, tips, social ranking | Cash desk to load time |
| VIP / VVIP rooms as status unlocks | Ban / whitelist, events, guest list |
| Staff tip pad & door tools | HR: create staff emails for demos |

Guests feel a premium club in their pocket. Operators get a control room for the night.

## What we can show today (MVP)

### Guest experience (phone)

- Age-gated membership and club identity  
- Time wallet that updates in real time  
- Entry / exit flow with QR for door staff  
- Active lounge: challenges, feed, cocktail menu that **deducts time**, rankings by club time  
- Social currency moments (pass the glass, tip the bartender)  
- Tiered status (Bronze → VVIP) based on time on account  

### Operator experience (web console)

Live at the admin console (hosted for demos):

- **Operations dashboard** — members, active visits, cash taken today  
- **Load Time** — POS-style bills; void a mistaken load  
- **Members & Access** — search, ban, whitelist  
- **Calendar & Events** + **Guest list** for themed nights  
- **HR** — create employee emails and temp passwords for door/bar staff demos  

### How a night works (simple story)

1. Guest arrives and pays cash at the desk  
2. Staff loads e.g. **₱2,000 → 2 hours** onto their account  
3. Guest’s phone updates immediately  
4. Door scans them in; lounge timer runs  
5. They order drinks or tip — time counts down with clear feedback  
6. House sees activity on the admin dashboard  

That is the core loop investors should feel in a walkthrough.

---

## What this is *not* yet (honest MVP note)

We are early on purpose. This build is an **MVP** to prove product and pilot operations.

**Still ahead (not finished):**

- Full **payment walls** (GCash / card checkout in production)  
- Hardened **security patches** and production compliance review  
- Mature fraud controls, audits, and enterprise access policies  
- Polished public marketing site and full brand rollout  

We are not asking investors to believe the stack is “finished banking software.”  
We are asking them to believe: **the night can run on time-as-currency, and the house can operate it.**

## Why this can matter commercially

- **New membership layer** — the club owns a digital relationship beyond a single door fee  
- **Higher engagement in-venue** — the phone stays part of the night, not a distraction  
- **Clear ops story** — cash today, digital payments when partners and controls are ready  
- **Status economy** — tiers and VIP rooms create reasons to load more time and come back  
- **Pilot path** — one real venue can validate spend, staffing, and guest reaction before scale

## What we’re asking investors to take away

1. **The insight** — nightlife needs a product that joins guest prestige with house control.  
2. **The wager** — *time* is a better unit than points alone for a club night.  
3. **The pragmatism** — cash-first MVP gets us into a real room faster than waiting on payments.  
4. **The honesty** — security depth and payment walls are planned next steps, not pretend-complete.  
5. **The ask behind the demo** — support taking this from demo → one live venue pilot → paid digital rails.

## Suggested live demo (10 minutes)

1. Show a **guest phone** with a low or empty wallet  
2. On **admin web**, load a standard bill onto that guest  
3. Watch the phone balance rise  
4. Enter lounge / order a drink — show time decrease  
5. Open **dashboard** and **events / guest list** for a themed night  
6. Create a **staff email** in HR and explain door vs bar roles  
7. Close with: *“Payments and deeper security are next — this proves the night can run.”*
