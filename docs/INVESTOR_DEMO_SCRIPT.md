# Investor Demo Script — The Blind Tiger

**Runtime:** ~3 minutes  
**Cast:** You (phone + story) · Partner or you on second device (admin desk)  
**Live admin:** https://blind-tiger-admin.vercel.app  
**Prep:** Member logged into the phone app · Admin logged into cash desk · Phone on loud enough for haptics (sound optional)

---

## Beat sheet

| Time | Surface | What you say | What you show |
|------|---------|--------------|---------------|
| **0:00–0:35** | Phone · Welcome | “Guests don’t open a utility app — they enter a private club.” | Swipe the 5 welcome scenes (Hero → Story → How → Loop → Access). Tap a How-card for the demo line. |
| **0:35–0:55** | Phone · Sign in | “Same door for members and staff. Age 21+.” | Sign in as the demo member. Land on pricing / pass. |
| **0:55–1:25** | Admin · Load Time | “Cash first. Staff loads a bill — the phone is the wallet.” | Cash desk → pick member → ₱2,000 (or similar) → confirm load. |
| **1:25–1:45** | Phone · Wallet hit | “Watch the currency land.” | Full-screen **TIME LOADED** celebration as balance climbs live. Pause one breath. |
| **1:45–2:10** | Phone · Entry | “Presence is the product. QR is the velvet door.” | Start visit / show entry QR · scan on staff (or narrate the door). |
| **2:10–2:40** | Phone · Lounge | “Minutes buy moments — drinks, tips, status.” | Lounge timer running · open menu or rankings briefly. |
| **2:40–3:00** | Close | “Time is the currency of the night. Ops on the web. Experience on the phone.” | Split view: admin dashboard pulse + phone lounge. Ask for the raise / next meeting. |

---

## One-liner (open)

> **The Blind Tiger** turns a night at the club into a living phone experience: guests buy **time**, spend it like currency, and the house runs the floor from a private command room.

## One-liner (close)

> We prove the loop cash-first — then layer digital payments when the night is already real.

---

## Demo checklist (before they sit down)

- [ ] Phone on the welcome / signed-in member with low or mid balance  
- [ ] Admin open on **Load Time (Cash)** with the member searchable  
- [ ] Know which bill you’ll load (₱2,000 → ~2h is the clean story)  
- [ ] Airplane mode **off** · both devices on the same story Wi‑Fi  
- [ ] Practice the silence after the wallet hit — don’t talk over it  

## If something glitches

| Glitch | Recovery |
|--------|----------|
| Wallet overlay doesn’t fire | Pull-to-refresh / re-open app · re-load a smaller bill · narrate “balance updates on `profiles` live” |
| QR / entry sticky | Use admin guest list or narrate door · jump to lounge if already inside |
| Admin slow | Have a second load ready · keep talking the cash-first thesis |

## Optional: welcome sound

In the welcome Access scene, toggle **Sound** on for a soft knock between scenes. Default is **off** (investor rooms stay quiet).
